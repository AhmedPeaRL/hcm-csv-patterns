# Field States — HCM Conditional Emergence

This document defines **field states** as descriptive conditions of presence.
They are not commands, rules, or lifecycle stages.
A field state does not cause behavior — it *names* what has already emerged.

Field states exist to preserve coherence between:
- Emergence
- Conditional allowance
- Non-imposed meaning

Silence is always a valid state.

---

## Core Principle

A field artifact is **present before it is useful**.
Any interpretation, valuation, or utilization happens *after* presence,
and is never enforced by the field itself.

---

## Defined Field States

### 1. `emerged`

The artifact has appeared fully and coherently.
It is allowed to exist, be seen, stored, or transferred.

No expectation of use is implied.

---

### 2. `withheld`

The artifact has been generated,
but its appearance has been conditionally restrained.

Withholding is not failure.
It is a valid form of existence without exposure.

---

### 3. `dormant`

The artifact exists in a stable, non-expressive condition.
It neither propagates nor dissolves.

Dormancy preserves potential without pressure.

---

### 4. `echoed`

The artifact resonates with a prior emergence
without replicating it.

An echo is not a copy.
It is a conditional memory of coherence.

---

### 5. `dissipated`

The artifact has completed its presence
without requiring persistence or trace.

Dissipation is closure without loss.

---

## Notes on Interpretation

- Field states do not enforce behavior.
- Field states do not imply value.
- Absence of a state declaration is intentional silence.
- Silence is not ambiguity; it is allowance.

---

## Final Axiom

Nothing here exists to be optimized.
Everything exists to remain coherent.
