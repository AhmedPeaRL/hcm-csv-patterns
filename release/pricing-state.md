# Pricing State

Pricing is not valuation.
Pricing is a symbolic boundary.

Possible states:
- free
- pay-what-you-want
- fixed-minimal
- withheld

Pricing is determined by:
- Artifact density
- Non-dependence on outcome
- Absence of urgency

An artifact priced at zero is not lesser.
An artifact priced higher is not superior.

Price does not represent worth.
Price represents **permission to exchange**.
